About the property

Description property

5 bed / 3 bath – on an exceptionally large & private site

AMOVE is delighted to bring this attractive, exceptionally private, 5 bed family home to the market, well situated in the hearth of the village of Kildangan. This tucked away, private home, has been meticulously maintained throughout, with polished wood floors, solid wood doors, a superb south / west facing garden and enjoy a super corner position in this sought after estate. This spacious property extends to 208sqm (2,239sq.ft), boasts an impressive B BER rating, with bright, lovely proportions, spacious rooms and is surrounded by green fields and the open countryside.

Accommodation comprises: Hall, Lounge, Sitting room, Kitchen / Breakfast room, Utility, Guest W.C, Office / 5th bedroom. UPSTAIRS, 4 large double bedrooms, with the master being ensuite and a house bathroom.

Located in this popular established estate, situated in the centre of Kildangan village No. 7 is a short walk from the local primary school, shops, pub and community centre. This home is just 60km from the M50 in Dublin, 10km from Kildare village, 5.4km from Monasterevin and 13km from Athy. For buyers commuting the M7 is just 10km away at Junction 13 and the train station is located in Kildare town (10km). The Irish National Stud & Gardens and the equestrian heartland of the Curragh and Moore Abbey Woods are all within easy reach of this lovely forever home.
